The mark: the house-and-cloud glyph, painted in the beacon so it follows every palette. Pair it with "fog" in Plus Jakarta Sans 600 (`text-[17px] font-semibold text-ink`, `gap-2.5`) for the wordmark. `className` sets the height and the width follows (`h-5` default; `h-9` where it stands alone). It takes no colour — it is always the beacon. Do not invent another mark.

## Examples

```jsx
<span className="flex items-center gap-2.5 text-[17px] font-semibold text-ink"><Logo />fog</span>
<Logo className="h-9" />
```
