One row of a `RowList`: `title` and its `meta` facts on the left, the row's own `actions` on the right, and `children` (a bar, an error) under the line.

## Examples

```jsx
<RowItem title="media" meta="nas.lan:/volume1/media → /mnt/nas/media" actions={<><Button size="sm">Edit</Button><Button size="sm" variant="danger">Remove</Button></>} />
```
