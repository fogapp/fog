A list of things a page manages — keys, shares, app passwords, people — in one veil with a hairline between rows. Fill it with `RowItem`s.

## Examples

```jsx
<RowList>
  <RowItem title="laptop" meta="Created 3 days ago · used 1 hour ago" actions={<Button size="sm" variant="danger">Revoke</Button>} />
</RowList>
```
