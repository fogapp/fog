fog

Turn any spare PC into your home server — apps, backups, and updates, all from one dashboard.

A computer to dedicatex86-64 with UEFI — a mini-PC, NUC, or old desktop. 24 GB disk minimum.
A USB stick4 GB or larger. It will be erased.
An Ethernet cableHome servers work best wired to your router.
1

Download the installer

One image, about 2 GB. It carries everything — installation never needs the internet.

Download fog-installer-x86_64.img for UEFI x86-64 machines

sha256 — see fog-installer-x86_64.img.sha256 beside the download

2

Flash it to the USB stick

Open balenaEtcher (free, all platforms), choose the downloaded image, choose your stick, and hit Flash.

Prefer the terminal? dd if=fog-installer-x86_64.img of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress works too — mind the target device.

3

Boot the server from it

Plug the stick and the Ethernet cable into the server machine and power it on. Most machines boot the stick automatically; otherwise tap the boot-menu key (usually F12, F10, or Esc) and pick the USB entry.

With a screen attached, the setup wizard appears right there. Headless? Open http://fog.local from your phone or laptop — same wizard.

4

Answer four questions

Name your server, pick the disk, create your account — a few minutes later you're on your dashboard, installing your first app. The stick becomes your recovery tool; keep it in a drawer.

Pull the stick out during the final restart. Leave it in and the machine boots the installer again — it recognizes the server it just built and says so, so nothing is at risk either way.

Already have a Fog backup?

The same installer restores a server: choose Restore from a backup on the first screen and hand it your encrypted recovery kit. It verifies the kit before touching any disk.

Fog is self-hosted — after this page, everything happens on your own hardware.
